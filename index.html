StudyFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

StudyFlow is a study planning app that helps users organize study sessions, calendar items, subjects, focus time, and goals. This policy explains how StudyFlow handles information.

Information We Collect

StudyFlow does not require an account and does not collect personal information on a server. Information you enter, such as subjects, calendar items, study sessions, notes, goals, and focus progress, is stored locally on your device.

How Information Is Used

Information saved in the app is used only to show your study schedule, sessions, focus timer, subjects, and progress inside StudyFlow.

Sharing

StudyFlow does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or third parties.

Data Storage

Study data is stored locally on your device. If you uninstall the app or clear app data, your saved study information may be deleted.

Children's Privacy

StudyFlow is designed for students and general study organization. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes To This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if StudyFlow adds new features or data handling practices. Updates will be shown on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact the StudyFlow app developer.

This privacy policy applies to the StudyFlow Android app and web version.
